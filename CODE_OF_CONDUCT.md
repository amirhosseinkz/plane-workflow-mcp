# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be welcoming, respectful, and free
from harassment for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and open to good-faith feedback.
- Focus discussion on ideas, behavior, and the work rather than personal
  characteristics.
- Respect privacy and do not share credentials, private data, or information
  about people or organizations without permission.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair harm when it occurs.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure
of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security/advisory
reporting channel or another private maintainer contact published in the
repository settings. Do not open a public issue for a sensitive report.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect the reporter's privacy where
possible, and may remove content, limit participation, or take other
proportionate action. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good
faith is not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces and to public behavior when it has a
meaningful impact on the project community.
